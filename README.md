The early identification of brain tumors is highly important due to its impact on patients' well being. It is one of the most common health issues throughout the world, and in this project, we use a pre-trained ResNet-18 model to classify MRI images using transfer learning.

In this project, we adapt the ResNet-18 to our medical image classification by training only the last fully connected layer of the model. This method improves efficiency and meets acceptable performance standards while still ensuring solid outcomes resulting from its reduced training time and computational costs.This project focuses on the classification of brain tumors from MRI scans into four categories:

1.Glioma

2.Meningioma

3.Pituitary Tumor

4.No Tumor
